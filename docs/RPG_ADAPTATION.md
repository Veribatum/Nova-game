# BLACKGLASS RPG adaptation · October 27, 2026

## The game

Nova is an independent mercenary captain who needs to keep Rapscallion flying. A small crew, a mixed-species port, authored contracts, tangible equipment improvements, and consequential deliveries give the frontier a life beyond a single infiltration level. Nova remains the protagonist. Abilities are technological.

The opening loop is **prepare aboard ship → choose paid work → explore a location → recover the deliverable → extract → settle the contract → improve equipment and relationships**. Friendly areas are safe for conversations. Hostile areas support shooting, cover, low-profile movement, and cloaking. The map reflects the playable level geometry.

## Prototype contracts

| Contract | Stated motive and deliverable | Activity | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paper Trail | A freight insurer needs an authenticated cargo seizure record. | UFD facility infiltration and archive recovery; maintenance alternative with a service transponder. | Normal full payment, a local receipt copy, and an unusual external authorization. No reactor sabotage or immediate conspiracy revelation. |
| Borrowed Fire | A settlement engineer needs a stolen thermal regulator. | Recover physical hardware from a Kharuun raider refinery. | Return it for the agreed fee and trust, or sell it to a private buyer for a larger payment and a reputation loss. |
| Dead Air | A salvage cooperative buys intact power cells. | Recover three distinct cells in a derelict survey ship. | A straightforward salvage fee. An optional survey log records an unexplained reading without supplying its cause. |

These are this RPG's prototype contracts. Their clients, place names, supporting NPCs, and second mission objective are new adaptation content, not amendments to the canonical Design Bible. The UFD opening and Kharuun second chapter follow the supplied setting direction; the ship-first onboarding serves the new RPG format. This repo does not reproduce or rearrange the Mac Godot level.

## What carries forward

Purchased weapons and suit modules, three ability paths, experience and levels, collected supplies, defeated enemies, discovered dossiers, contract stages, delivery choices, standing, and position persist. Salvage can be repeated; a new salvage claim resets only its own wreck resources and opposition. Payment is idempotent. Regulator disposition changes both a saved decision and numerical reputation.

## Mystery boundaries

Early evidence stays mundane enough to admit ordinary explanations. A repeated administrative office is a lead to keep, not proof of BLACKGLASS. Players do not receive the Ophenpop true-form reveal, the final answer to the Precursors, or a definitive explanation of disconnection in this chapter. Kharuun Ascension atrocities are not casually explained away. Vyraen skill is observation and signal analysis. Keshar integration expertise is not replaced by a new magical genetic ability.

## Art integration priorities

The first supplied asset pass should replace the provisional Kharuun combatants, the human/security cast, the navigator, and major ship/interior props. Nova's existing animated model is already present. The best next delivery is one archive of available characters and props, retaining embedded textures and original names. The developer should inspect the pack and perform conversion, clip mapping, scale normalization, sockets, and collision work; this should not become a request for the owner to author and upload every animation separately.

## Next expansion after actual play feedback

1. Validate visible Nova deformation, facing, grip, camera clearance, and performance on a WebGL-capable phone and desktop.
2. Integrate the owner's distinctive art and visually validate it in movement, combat, and close conversation.
3. Expand the port's social work and the crew's persistent reactions to delivery choices.
4. Add the next authored contract with a different surface problem, while keeping the larger mystery gradual.

Manual spaceflight and a full campaign are future scope. The initial build emphasizes a complete ground mission and homecoming loop.
